ABOUT OUR FARM

Our Story • Our Passion • Our Commitment

Welcome to Shod of Firebird, a farm that represents dedication, patience, responsibility, and the passion for raising healthy and well-maintained roosters.

Our farm is more than a place where animals are raised. It is a place where knowledge, experience, and responsibility come together. We believe that successful farming begins with proper management and genuine care for every animal.

Raising a healthy rooster requires consistency. It is not only about providing food and water. It also involves maintaining a clean environment, providing comfortable shelter, monitoring health, and understanding the different needs of animals as they grow.

Every day is an opportunity to improve our farming practices. From preparing balanced nutrition to cleaning the surroundings and observing animal behavior, every responsibility plays an important role in maintaining a healthy farm.

At Shod of Firebird, we believe that patience is one of the most important qualities in farming. Growth takes time, and good results come from consistent effort. We focus on responsible animal husbandry rather than shortcuts.

Our dream is to develop a farm recognized for responsible care, proper management, quality, and dedication. We want every experience to become a lesson and every challenge to become an opportunity to improve.

THE ART OF PROPER CARE

Building healthy growth through responsible management

01

Proper Nutrition

Balanced poultry feed provides important nutrients for healthy development. Choose feed suitable for the animal's age and nutritional requirements.

02

Fresh Water

Clean drinking water should always be available. Regularly wash drinking containers and prevent contamination.

03

Clean Environment

Keep the shelter dry, clean, and well-ventilated. Proper sanitation helps reduce the risk of diseases.

04

Health Monitoring

Observe appetite, activity, breathing, and behavior. Seek veterinary advice when an animal shows signs of illness.

05

Daily Management

Consistent daily attention helps ensure that animals receive proper food, water, shelter, and comfortable living conditions.

06

Preventive Care

Follow suitable vaccination and parasite prevention programs with guidance from a qualified poultry veterinarian.

THE JOURNEY OF GROWTH

Patience is the foundation of progress

Every rooster has its own growth journey. From its early development to adulthood, proper nutrition, clean surroundings, suitable housing, and regular health monitoring are essential.

We believe that healthy growth cannot be rushed. Animals need time, appropriate care, and a suitable environment to develop. Our responsibility is to provide these important needs every single day.

We also believe that success is not simply measured by winning or competition. True achievement comes from responsible farming, healthy animals, valuable experience, and the ability to continuously improve.

OUR FARM PHILOSOPHY

"A great farm is not built overnight. It is developed through patience, discipline, knowledge, and dedication."

At Shod of Firebird, we believe that every small responsibility matters. Good nutrition, clean water, proper shelter, and attention to animal health create the foundation of responsible farming.

We are committed to learning, improving, and maintaining high standards of animal care. Our pride comes from the effort we give and the responsibility we carry.
